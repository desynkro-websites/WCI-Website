About Us

A Family Business Built on Trust

From farmland to Perth backyards, our story is rooted in hard work, integrity, and pride in every job.

We founded WCI Digging & Excavation in 2012 as a natural extension of our lifelong experience working the land. Originally farmers and veterinary surgeons with over 25 years of experience operating heavy machinery, we decided to bring that expertise to Perth's excavation and earthmoving scene.

Today, it's still just us, myself Walter, my wife Carla, and our son Iago. We run every job personally, showing up on time, doing the work with care, and leaving every site clean and tidy. There are no salesmen or middlemen here. When you call WCI, you get the people who will be doing the digging.

We specialise in difficult-access and small excavation projects that bigger operators won't touch. With our ASV RT-60 compact track loader (3.25m long x 1.52m wide x 1.96m tall), Terex TC19 mini excavator (2.37m long x 0.99m wide x 2.36m tall), and an 8m³ tipper truck, we can handle jobs across Perth, from tight suburban backyards to larger commercial blocks.

What We Do

Our Services

From site prep to final clean-up, we handle the full scope of excavation and earthmoving.

General Excavation

Site preparation, digging, trenching, and earthmoving for residential and commercial projects across Perth.

Landscape Preparation

Lawn removal, soil levelling, site grading, and preparation ready for turf, paving, or garden design.

Concrete & Paving Removal

Demolition and removal of old concrete slabs, driveways, pathways, and pavers. Full clean-up included.

Shed Pads & Foundations

Compact, level shed pads and small foundation preparation for garden sheds, garages, and workshops.

Small Demolitions

Safe, controlled demolition of small structures, retaining walls, fences, and garden features. Tip-truck removal included.

Tipper Truck Service

Our 8m³ tipper truck means we haul away all waste on the spot. No skip bins needed, cleaner, faster, cheaper.
